    Help

    On iPhone

    1. Safari only (not Chrome). Share → Add to Home Screen.
    2. Allow Camera. Use the rear camera at the range.
    3. Calibrate on a known-size target at a known distance.
    4. Zoom with +/− or the slider. The reticle grows with zoom (FFP).
    5. Measure: pick two points → Δ mrad. Optional size if you enter range.

    Zoom limits (honest)

    iPhone Safari almost never exposes the Camera app’s 0.5× / 2× / 3× lenses or optical zoom to a website. This app uses digital zoom (the picture is enlarged on screen). Past about 4× the image gets soft. Native hardware zoom is used only if the browser reports it (uncommon on iOS).

    Safety

    Angular estimate only. Not a surveyed optic. Recalibrate after rotating the phone, flipping cameras, or changing cases/lenses. Verify any hold or size call with a known-distance target or a real scope before you rely on it.
